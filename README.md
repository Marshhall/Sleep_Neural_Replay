<h1>Sleep Neural Replay</h1>

<p>
  Investigating the reactivation of task-related neural patterns during sleep
  using recordings from 37 neurons in the rat prefrontal cortex.
</p>

<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>
  This notebook compares neural activity before, during, and after a
  maze-learning task. Principal component analysis (PCA) and random matrix
  theory help identify cell assemblies and measure their reactivation
  during sleep.
</p>

<h2>Methods</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Spike-train visualization and binning</li>
  <li>Correlation analysis and PCA</li>
  <li>Marchenko–Pastur spectrum and shuffled-data controls</li>
  <li>Comparison of pre-task and post-task replay</li>
</ul>

<h2>Tools</h2>
<p>Python, NumPy, SciPy, pandas, Matplotlib, and Jupyter.</p>

<h2>Reference</h2>
<p>
  Peyrache et al. (2009).
  <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.2337">
    Replay of rule-learning related neural patterns in the prefrontal
    cortex during sleep
  </a>.
</p>
